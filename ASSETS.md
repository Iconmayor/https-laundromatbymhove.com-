# Asset sources

The original meshes of garments, equipment and phones, the marble studio, and the illustrative surface comparisons and fallback video were produced for Laundromat by MHOVE in Blender. The supplied brand logos are used with their original geometry and colours. The reference videos supplied art and motion direction.

The phone screens are from the official [customer](https://apps.apple.com/ng/app/laudromat/id6826253623) and [vendor](https://apps.apple.com/ng/app/laudromat-vendor/id6826253908) listings. App download badges point to the publisher's App Store and Google Play listings.

Service photography follows the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The photos depict service categories and are separate from the illustrated comparison scenes.
